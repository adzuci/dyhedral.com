Topo

The look of a climbing guidebook: text first, drawn in line, labelled like a map. Cool granite paper, spruce ink, and one lichen signal.

01 / Principles
01

Text first

The words do the work. Layout is a column, a rule and space. If a page works as plain text, it will work designed.

02

One signal

Lichen appears once per screen, on the thing that matters most. Everything else is ink and granite.

03

Labelled like a map

Small mono captions say what a thing is: a number, a grade, a unit, a status. No decoration without information.

04

Drawn, not rendered

Pictures are line diagrams in ink. No stock photos, no 3D, no gradients, no generated imagery.

02 / Logo
dyhedral
dyhedral

The mark is two faces meeting on a line: the corner in ink, the route in lichen. The wordmark is lowercase Instrument Sans 700 at -4% tracking. Keep clear space equal to the mark's width on every side. Do not recolour, outline, rotate or set the name in capitals.

07 / Do & don't

Do

  • Lead with the sentence, then design around it.
  • Use lichen once per screen.
  • Separate with rules and space before reaching for a box.
  • Label with numbers, grades and units.
  • Keep a landing page under 200 words.

Don't

  • Gradients, glows, glass or blur.
  • Purple, indigo or violet.
  • Cream paper with orange, or serif headlines.
  • Pill buttons, card grids or soft shadows.
  • Sparkle icons, emoji bullets or a centred prompt box.
